Arrow Draft

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 September 2026

Arrow Draft has no accounts and never asks for your name, email, or any other personal detail. It does show advertising, and it uses Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played. This page explains exactly what that involves and how to control it.

Information we collect

We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, or precise location, and there is no account to create or sign in to. The app requests no runtime permissions beyond vibration, which is used only for in-game haptic feedback and can be turned off in Settings.

Two Google services do collect limited data on our behalf:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase records anonymous gameplay events — a level being opened, a level being completed and its star rating, and which screen you are on — along with the standard information Firebase collects automatically, such as a random app-instance identifier, device model, operating system version, and approximate (country-level) region. This tells us which levels are too hard and where players stop playing. It is not linked to your identity.
  • Google AdMob serves the ads and may use your device's advertising ID and ad interaction data to select ads, measure their performance, and limit how often the same ad is shown. Whether ads are personalised depends on your consent choice — see Advertising and consent below.

Information stored on your device

The game saves your progress locally so you can pick up where you left off. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted anywhere:

  • which levels you have completed, and your star rating for each
  • the level you are currently on, including a part-played board
  • your settings: sound, vibration, and dark mode
  • a flag recording that you have seen the introduction
  • the date you first opened the app, used only to decide whether an initial ad-free period applies

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Network use

Arrow Draft requires an internet connection to play. The app makes these kinds of network request:

  • a periodic connectivity check, used to tell you when your device is offline. It asks a well-known "no content" address (clients3.google.com/generate_204, and cloudflare.com as a fallback) and looks only at whether a reply arrives. No data about you or your play is included.
  • analytics events sent to Google Analytics for Firebase
  • a configuration fetch from Firebase Remote Config, which downloads our advertising settings. It sends no information about you.
  • ad requests to Google AdMob, plus the impression and click reporting that goes with them
  • a one-time consent message fetch, where privacy law requires us to ask for your choice

Advertising and consent

Ads in Arrow Draft are served by Google AdMob. You will see banner ads on the level select screen and in a strip below the game board, an occasional full-screen ad between levels, and an ad in the panel shown when you finish a level or in the box that asks you to confirm leaving or restarting a level. There is also an optional "watch a video for more lives" ad, which only ever plays if you choose to tap it.

We may keep the game ad-free for a short period after you first install it. The length of that period can change without an app update, and may be none.

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or another region where consent is required, you will be asked once whether you agree to personalised advertising. Declining does not remove ads — it means the ads you see are non-personalised, chosen without using your ad interaction history. You can change this choice at any time from Settings → Ad Privacy Choices inside the app.

Google may use your device's advertising ID for this. You can reset or delete that ID in your Android settings under Privacy → Ads. For details on how Google handles this data, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services and the list of advertising technology providers.

Third-party services

The app uses the following Google services, and no others. There is no crash reporting, and no social or login integration.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase — anonymous gameplay analytics
  • Firebase Remote Config — downloads our advertising settings; collects nothing about you
  • Google AdMob — serves the advertising described above

All three are operated by Google under the Google Privacy Policy and the Firebase privacy documentation. If you install the app from Google Play, Google also collects installation and device data under its own policy, which we do not control.

Children's privacy

Arrow Draft is a general-audience game. It is not directed at children, is not part of Google Play's Designed for Families programme, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Because the app is not child-directed, its advertising is not configured for child-directed treatment. If you believe a child has provided information through this app, contact us at the address below and we will delete it.

Purchases

The current version contains no in-app purchases and no payment processing. Should that change, this policy will be updated before the feature ships.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be published at this same address with a new "last updated" date. Material changes will also be noted in the app's release notes on Google Play.
